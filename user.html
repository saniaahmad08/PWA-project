About Bloom Buddies

Bloom Buddies is an educational Progressive Web App (PWA) built with HTML5, CSS, and JavaScript. It is designed to help users learn about different types of flowers in a fun and interactive way. The app features images, descriptions, audio narration, and growing instructions for each flower. It can be installed on any device and works fully offline once installed.

Features

  • Flower Menu — a sidebar menu lists all available flowers. Click any flower to learn about it.
  • Images — each flower has a high quality photo displayed alongside its description.
  • Descriptions — read a short educational description about each flower.
  • Audio Narration — press play on the audio player to hear the flower's description read aloud.
  • Grow Your Own — click the green button to expand a section with growing tips including sunlight, water, soil type, best season, and a helpful tip.
  • Install as App — install Bloom Buddies on your phone or desktop to use it like a real app.
  • Works Offline — once installed, the app works even without an internet connection.

How to Use the App

  1. Open the app in your browser.
  2. You will see a list of flowers in the sidebar on the left.
  3. Click on any flower name to view its image, description, and audio player.
  4. Press the play button on the audio player to hear the description.
  5. Click the Grow Your Own! button to see growing instructions.
  6. Click the button again to close the growing instructions.

How to Install

  1. Open Bloom Buddies in Google Chrome on your phone or computer.
  2. Look for the Install App button at the top of the page.
  3. Click it and select Install when the browser prompts you.
  4. The app will be added to your home screen or desktop.
  5. Open it anytime — even without an internet connection!

Note: The install button only appears in Google Chrome when visiting the live hosted version of the app.
